Research behind the rules
  • Public backtests of BTC (2017 bull run, 2023, and a 49-strategy test on Binance data) found single-indicator RSI and MACD often lag buy-and-hold or lose; EMA crossover and breakout trend-following ranked best, but mostly in a strong bull market.
  • High win-rate mean-reversion setups (RSI, Bollinger) showed severe drawdowns. Win rate matters less than average win vs average loss.
  • Gold vendors and researchers favor 4H/Daily trend-following: a long-term MA filter, breakout or pullback entries, ATR volatility filter, ATR trailing stop. Published gold results are vendor claims, not verified.
  • Risk practice: 1% of equity per trade, stop at ~2.5x ATR, no fixed take-profit, let a trailing stop exit.
  • This app combines those ideas: trend filter (EMA 50/200), entry on 20-bar breakout or RSI pullback in trend, volatility filter, 2.5x ATR stop, trailing stop, 1% sizing.

Trend systems win maybe 35–45% of the time and endure long losing streaks in sideways markets. Backtests came from public posts with bull-market bias, so expect weaker live results.

⚠️ Educational tool, not financial advice. Signals are math on past prices and will often be wrong. Paper-trade first and risk only what you can afford to lose. Gold uses PAXG (a gold-backed token), which can differ slightly from spot gold.
