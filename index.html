How it works
  • Regime: ADX, efficiency ratio and Choppiness Index (CHOP, 61.8 = choppy) decide trending, ranging or in between.
  • Trending: 20-bar breakout or RSI pullback, EMA trend filter, higher-timeframe agreement, ATR stop with trailing exit.
  • Ranging: buy below the lower Bollinger band with RSI under 30 (sell above upper band with RSI over 70). Target is the middle band, stop 1.5×ATR, exit early if the market starts trending.
  • Sniper: price sweeps the 20-bar low (or high), then a candle closes back inside with a rejection wick. Stop just beyond the wick, target 2R. In trending markets only with the trend.
  • In between or news day: no trade.

Sweep and zone setups come mostly from tutorials, not independent testing. Trust them only if the backtest agrees on more than one timeframe and asset. 15m and 30m backtests cover just days of data, so they are noisy.

⚠️ Educational tool, not financial advice. Signals are math on past prices and will often be wrong. Paper-trade first and risk only what you can afford to lose. Gold uses PAXG, which can differ from spot gold.
